# NARRATIVE & AUDIO BIBLE: FUN UNCLE

> **Genre:** Mockumentary Sports-Action Comedy  
> **Comedic Lineage:** *The Office*, *Spinal Tap*, *The Big Lebowski*, *Shaolin Soccer*, *30 for 30*

---

## 1. Narrative Framing: The Accidental Odyssey

The entire game is presented as raw, unedited footage from an upcoming documentary film titled:  
**"THE HEAVINESS OF BEING: The Raymond Chronicles"**  
*Directed & Narrated by Vance Sterling*

### The Core Conflict
The uncle doesn't want to save the world, conquer a syndicate, or win a sports cup. He is driven entirely by two everyday domestic responsibilities:
1. **Rescuing his niece Maya** before his sister finds out he lost her on his watch.
2. **Finishing Aunt Brenda's hardware store errand** before the pot roast gets cold at 5:45 PM.

## 2. Dynamic Dialogue & Commentary System

The game features three distinct layers of voiceover that overlap dynamically during gameplay:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  LAYER 1: VANCE STERLING (Pretentious Literary Biographer Voiceover)   │
│  "He descended like an aging comet wrapped in polyester..."           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LAYER 2: AUNT BRENDA (Ticking Clock Phone Calls over Comms)           │
│  "Ray, did you get the 2-inch or 3-inch zinc screws?"                  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LAYER 3: FUN UNCLE (Deadpan In-Game Grunts & Coaching Advice)          │
│  "All in the wrist, boys. You're gripping too tight."                  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 4. The "Talking Head" Confessionals (Micro-Cutaways)

After catastrophic wipeouts, district boss defeats, or major mission milestones, the action instantly cuts for **4 seconds** to Fun Uncle seated in a folding aluminum lawn chair holding a lukewarm canned beverage:

* **Example 1 (After crashing through an executive office):**
  > *"Look, did I know that was the CEO's personal antique mahogany desk? No. But did it have the exact right ramp angle to launch me onto the monorail? Absolutely."*
* **Example 2 (After dogfighting patrol drones):**
  > *"Kids these days rely too much on radar. In my day, you looked out the side window and you checked your blind spot. It's common courtesy."*

## 5. Audio & Radio Direction

### The Radio Stations (Accessible while on skateboard, scooter, bike, or glider)
* **K-DICK 104.5 "The Yacht":** Smooth 1970s and 80s yacht rock, electric piano grooves, and mellow California harmonies (Doobie Brothers / Steely Dan vibes).
* **FUNK DISPATCH 92.1:** High-tempo slap bass, horn sections, and 70s urban street funk.
* **REC-ROOM FM:** 90s cassette-tape skate punk and garage rock.

### In-Game Sound Design
* **The Skate Audio:** Deep tactile urethane wheel rolls, concrete cracks, crisp metal-on-steel rail grinds, and shoe-sole grip tape scuffs.
* **The Paddle Audio:** Sharp, authentic hollow celluloid ping-pong *pock* sounds amplified with sonic sub-bass booms on power smashes.
* **The Stiff-Arm Audio:** Heavy leather-on-pad thud followed by ceramic armor shattering and metallic bowling-pin knockdowns.
